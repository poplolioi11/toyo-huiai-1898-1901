# 廣州惠愛醫癲院首期報告（1898–1901） / First Report of the Refuge for the Insane at Canton (1898–1901) — Transcription Dataset

廣州惠愛醫癲院首期英文報告涵蓋 1898 年 2 月至 1901 年 8 月，載有病人個案、逐名入院登記、院務、捐款，以及嘉約翰（John G. Kerr）紀念文。病歷和登記表可對讀院方如何記載入院與離院。

The first report of the Refuge for the Insane at Canton covers February 1898 through August 1901. It includes individual patient narratives, a register, accounts, lists of donations, and a biographical sketch of founder John G. Kerr.

## Source and scope

Toyo Bunko, Morrison Pamphlets, P-III-a-1898, IIIF frames 1–38. Original imprint: Canton, China. Two editorial omissions are marked at scan 16, printed page 20, and scan 25, printed page 34.

## Files

- `transcription.txt` retains printed lines, with editorial frame and leaf markers. Tabs mark table cells.
- `transcription.json` identifies each frame, leaf and text or table unit. Left and right follow the scan. A null printed page means the leaf has no verified printed page number. Use the source links to locate the underlying image.
- `transcription.tei.xml` maps the same page text and table cells into TEI P5.
- `review-notes.md` describes review coverage and reading questions.
## Transcription and review

AI-assisted reading used the fixed scans. Printed spelling, typographical anomalies and numeric values are retained. Spaces are normalized. Illustrations are represented by their surviving captions and labels. A printed total may differ from the sum of its rows. A square (□) means a reading is unresolved. See the notes for locations.

Version 0.1.0. This dataset has no measured error rate or expert proofreading.
