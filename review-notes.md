# Review notes

Version 0.1.0. IIIF frames 1–38.

Initial edition with two clearly marked editorial omissions. This issue covers February 1898 through August 1901. The report is associated with John G. Kerr and ends with a biography signed C. R. Hager followed by a compiler inscription for M. N. Kerr. The first and last scan in this issue are 1 and 38 in Toyo Bunko's composite item.

- Scans 10–14 contain the patient narratives and photographs. Scan 10, page 9, prints “Wo Cheuk”; scan 18, page 23, prints “Ho Cheuk.” Each is transcribed where printed.
- Scans 18–19 contain the register with 135 continuous entries. The columns and their printed notes are recorded in source order. A blank original cell is distinct from the square used for an unclear reading.
- The public transcript marks omitted passages at scan 16, left leaf, printed page 20, and scan 25, left leaf, printed page 34. These portions are excluded from the transcript and are represented as gaps.
- The figures in the accounts are transcribed as printed. Apparent arithmetic discrepancies are kept for source criticism.

The transcription was prepared with AI assistance and compared with the source images. Expert and human proofreading are pending.
